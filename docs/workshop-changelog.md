0.4.1

Bug fixes for Fire on my lead, Stealth and squads you take over.

FIXED

- Fire on my lead: men walking a route or patrol keep holding fire past each waypoint; switching to a weapon with fewer rounds loaded or reloading no longer counts as your first shot; and a man given a new order mid-fight (Move here, Halt, Follow) goes back to his earlier fire rules afterwards instead of firing at will for good.
- Stealth: leaving it no longer leaves return fire on for good, and a fire rule you gave while in Stealth is kept when you switch to Aware, Safe or Combat.
- A squad you took over keeps its commander's objective through fire-rule, stance, engine, lights, Heal up, Rearm, ammo and bandage, Take weapon, seat change, Unflip and Suppress orders. It is replaced only once a man actually carries out your order, so a Take cover that finds no cover or a Garrison with no free post leaves it in place.
- In a squad you took over, an order to selected men that covers every living man counts as a whole-squad order; an order to only some of them keeps the commander's objective.
- Eject: a man the game won't let bail out keeps his orders, and the reply no longer claims nobody was in a vehicle.
