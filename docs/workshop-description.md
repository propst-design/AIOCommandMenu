AiO Command Menu (Reforger)

Your squad. Your orders.

Press T and use the number keys, or the mouse, to move your AI squad, split it into colour teams, manage vehicles and put specialists to work. An unofficial standalone Reforger rebuild based on Leopard20's All-In-One Command Menu for Arma 3.

PUT YOUR SQUAD TO WORK

- Command the whole squad, selected soldiers or colour teams. Set formations, stance, speed, rules of engagement and Safe, Aware, Combat or Stealth behaviour.
- Bound forward with covering soldiers, peel back or send a team around a flank; in contact, men with smoke screen the move.
- Garrison a building, each man at a window facing out, or clear it room by room. Watch a direction or form an outward-facing ring.
- Throw frags or smoke, or lay a smoke screen, where you aim, using the grenades your men carry.
- Board vehicles and static weapons, choose seats, control engines and lights, dismount passengers. Send soldiers to unflip a vehicle or repair it with a carried repair kit.
- Rearm, pick up a weapon, heal up or send a squadmate to treat a casualty with real supplies. Ask for spare ammo or bandages.
- Plant a timed or detonator charge, lay a mine or disarm mines with an equipped soldier.
- Have a squad soldier fire a nearby mortar: HE, smoke or illumination.
- Plan infantry routes and looping patrols. A stalled route keeps its plan: resume it or skip a waypoint.
- Become leader of your squad, even from an AI leader while possessing a squad member, or step down.

SEE WHERE YOUR ORDERS LAND

While the menu is open, the aim marker shows where an aimed order will land and how far away it is: the building Garrison and Clear would use, or the vehicle your men would get into, unflip or repair. It turns grey when there is nothing there. Prefer old-Arma controls? More > Mouse control lets the wheel move through the menu, left click give the order and right click go back. Your menu settings are remembered across missions.

GET STARTED

1. Enable AiO and lead a player squad with linked AI. In Game Master, take control of a soldier to command the other AI in that group.
2. Press T. Use 1-9 to choose a line; 0 opens Select members. Backspace goes back, or clears selection on Select members.
3. Choose an order. No selection means the whole squad.
4. Aim at a destination, or open the map and put the cursor there. Choosing the order uses that spot at once.

Rebind T under Settings > Controls > All-in-One Command Menu. Selection expires about eight seconds after the menu closes; check the recipients in the heading. Colour teams last for the mission.

The squad bar shows names, teams, health, magazines and tasks while the menu is open (More > Squad bar on/off). A compact status stays on screen with the menu closed for recipients, routes, paused bounds and treatment.

Admin commands need server permission (-aioCheats admins, all or off): heal, teleport yourself, your squad or your vehicle with everyone aboard.

REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITS

Requires only Arma Reforger (checked on 1.9). Load AiO on the server and joining clients; it is not client-only.

AiO commands your existing squad. It does not recruit soldiers or command arbitrary groups from the GM camera. The game's own command wheel overrides AiO orders, and a soldier a player or Game Master takes over is left to them.

Buildings, doors and vehicle routes depend on Reforger's navigation: Clear building does not guarantee every room is cleared, and Fortify here finds cover rather than building it. Specialist orders need the right carried kit, ammunition and a reachable target.

Better Battle Buddies is optional; compatible builds lower weapons on quiet travel. Combining AiO with another squad-command menu mod, including Enhanced AI Squad Commands, is unsupported.
