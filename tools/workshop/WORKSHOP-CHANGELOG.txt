0.3.0 - Formations, routes and squad coordination

- Added Move > Formation: Column, Staggered column, Wedge and Line using Reforger's native formations. Applies to the whole squad and persists for later moves.
- Moved Speed to Stance & speed > Speed.
- Added More > Routes: plan up to 16 infantry waypoints from the map cursor or camera target, then hold at the end or repeat as a patrol. Select members or a colour team before adding the first point.
- Added queued arrival and stalled-movement popups for routes and ordinary Move here, visible with the menu closed. Arrival waits for the surviving assigned men; patrols stay quiet each lap.
- Added Defense > Watch that direction and Watch: own choice. Men already holding keep their posts.
- Added Supplies > Treat that soldier and Treat me: an available squad member uses real medical supplies and native treatment.
- Added 360 defense radii: Auto, 5, 10, 20, 30 and 50 m.
- Infantry retain movement destinations and posts during contact, fire from posts, and resume after immediate danger or available self-treatment. Engage at will releases a commanded post.
- Replaced timed Bound forward with Bound to position: two elements alternate moves until they reach the map/camera destination. Peel back also advances by actual arrivals.
- Bounding checks covering readiness, pauses for pinning or aid needs, and reports blocked movement or heavy losses. Use Attack > Resume bound / peel when a manual resume is required.
- Ordinary Move here and route travel support optional BBB relaxed marching. AiO remains standalone; BBB is not required.
- Added a GitHub bug-report link to the Workshop description.
