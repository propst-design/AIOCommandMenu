AiO Command Menu (Reforger)

A fast, numbered command menu for the AI soldiers you lead, in the spirit of Leopard20's All-in-One Command Menu for Arma 3. Press T, then a number or two, and your squad moves, flanks, takes cover or clears a building. No mouse, no wheel.

Works anywhere you lead AI: Conflict, Game Master and Arma Commander Actual. Standalone, no other mods needed. Server side and client side: everyone on the server needs it (the Workshop downloads it for them).

CONTROLS
- T opens and closes the menu (rebind: Settings > Controls > All-in-One Command Menu).
- 1-9 pick a line. 0 = Select members on the top page, Back on the others. Backspace = back.
- While the menu is open the number keys only drive the menu, so you never switch weapons by accident.
- Orders go to the whole squad, or only to the men you pick on Select members (toggle men 1-7, 8 next page, 9 Teams).
- Colour teams: Red, Green, Blue, Yellow. Select a team, or put the selected men in one.
- Orders that need a place use the spot you are looking at, or the map cursor while the map is open. The menu works with the map open.
- While the menu is open a squad bar shows each man's number, health, magazines and current task, and his number floats over his head in his team colour.

THE MENU
1 Move: Move here, Follow me, Advance, Stay back, Flank left, Flank right (they keep station about 50 m from you and move with you), Fall back, Hold here, Formation (Column, Staggered column, Wedge, Line; always the whole squad)
2 Attack: Advance, Flank left, Flank right, Assault, Suppress, Fire on my lead, Bound to position, Peel back, Resume bound / peel
3 Vehicles: Get in as driver/gunner/passenger, Switch seat, Get out, Disembark passengers, Eject, Engine and light controls, Unflip
4 Defense: Take cover (each man finds cover facing the threat ahead of you), Garrison building, Clear building, 360 defense (Auto, 5, 10, 20, 30 or 50 m radius), Fortify here, Watch that direction, Watch: own choice
5 R.O.E.: Fire at will, Return fire only, Hold fire, Engage at will, Disengage
6 Stance & speed: Stand, Crouch, Prone, Auto, Copy my stance, Speed (walk, run, sprint, auto)
7 Supplies: Heal up, Rearm (from bodies, loose gear, vehicles, crates and arsenals nearby), Rearm from that, Take that weapon, Treat that soldier, Treat me
8 More: squad bar on/off, Routes, Admin page
9 HALT
0 Select members

SQUAD COORDINATION
- Routes: select men or a colour team, then More > Routes > Add waypoint here at each map/camera destination (up to 16). The first point fixes the draft's members. Start route holds at the end; Start patrol loops two or more points. Clear draft leaves active orders alone. Infantry only.
- Arrival/status popups work with the menu closed and queue so simultaneous reports remain readable. Arrival waits for the surviving assigned men. Routes report stalls; patrols stay quiet each lap.
- Bound to position alternates two elements until they reach your map/camera destination. Peel back alternates over 50 m. Cover readiness, pinning and aid needs can pause movement; blocked movement or heavy losses require Resume bound / peel. Follow, Halt or a new movement order cancels it.
- Infantry keep their destinations and posts during contact, fire from posts, and resume after immediate danger or available self-treatment. Engage at will releases a commanded post.
- Watch that direction keeps existing posts and faces your map/camera target; Watch: own choice removes the facing order. Native combat and danger can still draw attention.
- Treat that soldier / Treat me uses a nearby available selected helper, or the squad if nobody is selected. Helper and patient must be on foot; the helper must be conscious, have medical supplies and be within 200 m. Uses real items and native treatment. Follow or new movement cancels it.

ADMIN PAGE (More > Admin)
Heal me, Heal my squad, Teleport me, Teleport squad to me, Kill what I look at.
Always available in single player and to the host of a hosted game. On dedicated servers only logged-in admins get it. Server owners can change this with the start parameter -aioCheats all|admins|off.

COMPATIBILITY
- Your squad's voice replies come from the game's own commanding system.
- Tested alongside Arma Commander Actual and its AI mod Better Battle Buddies. Ordinary Move here and route travel support optional BBB relaxed marching; AiO works without it.
- Key bindings are added on top of the game's, so it stacks with other mods that add controls.
- Not meant to be combined with other squad-command menu mods.
