AiO Command Menu (Reforger)

Your squad. Your orders.

Press T and use the number keys to move your AI squad, split it into colour teams, manage vehicles and put specialists to work. An unofficial standalone Reforger rebuild based on Leopard20's All-In-One Command Menu for Arma 3.

PUT YOUR SQUAD TO WORK

- Command the whole squad, selected soldiers or colour teams. Set formations, stance, speed and rules of engagement.
- Choose Safe, Aware, Combat or Stealth behaviour.
- Bound forward with covering soldiers, peel back or send a team around a flank; in contact, men with smoke screen the move.
- Garrison a building, each man at a window post facing out, or clear it room by room. Watch a direction or form an outward-facing ring.
- Throw frags or smoke, or lay a smoke screen, where you aim, using the grenades your men carry.
- Become leader of your own squad, including from an AI leader while possessing a squad member, or step down.
- Board vehicles and static weapons, choose seats, control engines/lights and dismount passengers. Send soldiers to unflip a vehicle or repair it with a carried repair kit.
- Rearm, collect a weapon, heal up or send a squadmate to treat a casualty using real supplies. Ask for spare ammo or bandages.
- Send an equipped soldier to plant a timed or detonator-controlled charge, lay a mine or disarm mines around a point.
- Order a squad soldier to fire a nearby mortar: HE, smoke or illumination. It needs suitable shells nearby.
- Plan infantry routes and looping patrols. A stalled route keeps its plan: resume or skip a waypoint under More > Routes. Compact status shows progress and blocked soldiers.

GET STARTED

1. Enable AiO and lead a player squad with linked AI. In Game Master, take control of a soldier to command the other AI in that group.
2. Press T. Use 1-9 to choose a line; 0 opens Select members from the top page. Backspace goes back, or clears selection on Select members.
3. Choose an order. No selection means the whole squad. Most orders close the menu.
4. Aim at a destination, or open the map and place the cursor there. Choosing the order uses that position immediately; no extra confirmation click is needed.

Rebind T under Settings > Controls > All-in-One Command Menu. Number keys operate the open menu. Selection expires after about eight seconds with it closed; check the recipients in the heading. Formations and squad admin commands affect the whole squad. Colour teams last for the mission.

The menu-only squad bar shows names, teams, health, magazines and tasks. More > Squad bar on/off toggles it and nearby soldier numbers. Compact status remains visible with the menu closed for recipients, routes, paused bounds/peels and commanded treatment. Behaviour modes appear in the squad bar.

Admin commands require server permission. Teleport your squad with relative spacing, or your vehicle with everyone aboard. Teleport to my map cursor requires the map open: drivers bring their vehicle and occupants; passengers and gunners teleport out alone.

REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITS

Requires Arma Reforger, with no other mod dependencies. Load AiO on the server and joining clients; it is not client-only.

AiO commands your existing squad. It does not recruit soldiers or command arbitrary groups from the GM camera. Nearby friendly AI are not automatically yours.

Buildings, doors and vehicle routes depend on Reforger's navigation. Clear building does not guarantee every room or enemy is cleared. Fortify here finds cover; it does not construct fortifications.

Bounding needs ready cover and can pause under suppression, casualties or blocked movement. Use Attack > Resume bound / peel when requested; Follow or a new movement order regroups the men. Specialist orders require the right carried kit, available ammunition and a reachable target; repairs follow the kit's limits.

BBB is optional. Compatible builds can lower weapons on quiet ordinary travel; tactical orders retain readiness. Combining AiO with another squad-command menu mod, including Enhanced AI Squad Commands, is unsupported.
