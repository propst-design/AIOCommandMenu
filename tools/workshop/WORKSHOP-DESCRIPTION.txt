AiO Command Menu (Reforger)

Your squad. Your orders.

Press T and use the number keys to move your AI squad, split it into colour teams, manage vehicles, take cover or resupply. An unofficial standalone Reforger rebuild based on Leopard20's All-In-One Command Menu for Arma 3.

PUT YOUR SQUAD TO WORK

- Move the whole squad, selected soldiers or colour teams. Set formations, stance, speed and rules of engagement.
- Push forward with arrival-driven bounding, peel back under cover, or send a team around a flank.
- Garrison a building, clear reachable interior positions, watch a direction or form an outward-facing defensive ring.
- Board vehicles and static weapons, choose seats, control engines/lights, dismount passengers or send soldiers to unflip a vehicle.
- Rearm, collect a weapon, heal up or send an available squadmate to treat a casualty using real medical supplies.
- Plan infantry routes and looping patrols. Stalled routes keep their plan: use More > Routes to resume or skip a waypoint. A compact HUD shows progress and blocked soldiers.

GET STARTED

1. Enable AiO and lead a player squad with linked AI. In Game Master, take control of a soldier to command the other AI in that group.
2. Press T. Use 1–9 to choose a line; 0 opens Select members from the top page. Backspace goes back, or clears selection on Select members.
3. Choose an order. No selection means the whole squad. Most orders close the menu.
4. For destinations, aim your camera or open the map and put the cursor where you want the order. Choosing the order uses that position immediately; there is no extra confirmation click.

Rebind T under Settings > Controls > All-in-One Command Menu. While the menu is open, number keys operate it instead of weapon/quick-slot selection.

Selection expires after about eight seconds with the menu closed; check the recipients in the heading before giving another order. Formations and squad admin commands always affect the whole squad. Colour-team assignments last for the current mission.

The full squad bar shows names, team colours, health, magazines and tasks while the menu is open. More > Squad bar on/off toggles it and nearby soldier numbers. Compact status remains visible with the menu closed, showing recipients, route progress, paused bound/peel reasons and commanded treatment.

Admin commands appear only when the server permits them. Teleport your squad to a target with relative spacing, or teleport your vehicle with everyone aboard. Teleport to my map cursor requires the map open: drivers bring their vehicle and occupants; passengers and gunners teleport out alone.

REQUIREMENTS

Requires Arma Reforger, with no other mod dependencies. Load AiO on the server and joining clients; it is not client-only.

KNOW YOUR SQUAD'S LIMITS

AiO commands your existing squad: it does not recruit soldiers or command arbitrary groups from the Game Master camera. Nearby friendly AI are not automatically yours.

Buildings, doors and vehicle routes still depend on Reforger's navigation. Clear building visits sampled reachable positions; it does not promise every room or enemy is cleared. Fortify here finds cover; it does not construct fortifications.

Bounding needs ready covering soldiers and can pause under suppression, casualties or blocked movement. Use Attack > Resume bound / peel when requested. Follow or a new movement order lets you regroup.

BBB is optional. Compatible BBB builds can lower weapons during quiet ordinary travel; tactical orders retain readiness. Combining AiO with another squad-command menu mod, including Enhanced AI Squad Commands, is unsupported.
